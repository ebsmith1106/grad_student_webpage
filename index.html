About

Hello! My name is Elizabeth Bayly (EB) Smith. I am a graduate student in the Earth and Atmospheric Science Department at The City College of New York. I am also a NOAA EPP Center for Earth Systems Sciences and Remote Sensing Technologies II, Cohort 5 Fellow. I have my B.S. in Atmospheric Science and a minor in Mathematics from the University at Albany, SUNY. My work sits at the intersection of operational meteorology, data analysis, and emergency management.

Alongside my scientific training, I served as a volunteer EMT, which shapes how I think about forecasting: a forecast matters most when it helps people make decisions. I am interested in how weather information is produced, communicated, and acted on during high-impact events.

Research interests: weather risk communication · disaster preparedness · operational forecasting · atmospheric data analysis
