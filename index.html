About

I am a graduate student in atmospheric science with a B.S. in Atmospheric Science and a minor in Mathematics from the University at Albany, SUNY. My work sits at the intersection of operational meteorology, data analysis, and public safety.

Alongside my scientific training, I serve as a volunteer firefighter and EMT, which shapes how I think about forecasting: a forecast matters most when it helps people make decisions. I am interested in how weather information is produced, communicated, and acted on during high-impact events.

Research interests: weather risk communication · disaster preparedness · operational forecasting · atmospheric data analysis
