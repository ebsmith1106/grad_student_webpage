About

My work sits at the intersection of operational meteorology, data analysis, and emergency management.

Hello! My name is Elizabeth Bayly (EB) Smith. I am a graduate student in the Earth and Atmospheric Science Department at The City College of New York, and a NOAA EPP Center for Earth Systems Sciences and Remote Sensing Technologies II (CESSRST II), Cohort 5 Fellow. Research & Projects

Undergraduate research · 2026

MJO Lead–Lag Relationship with North Atlantic Tropical Storm Formation

Can today's Madden–Julian Oscillation phase hint at Atlantic storm formation weeks ahead? For 2009–2025, storms formed about twice as often when the MJO had been in phases 1–2 twelve days earlier as when it had been in phases 6–7.

Read the project →

Python · pandas · Statistical analysis · HURDAT2

Undergraduate honors thesis · University at Albany

Environmental and Structural Drivers of Hurricane Edouard (2014) Intensification

Combining P-3 tail Doppler radar, dropsondes, satellite imagery and lightning data to examine what helped Edouard strengthen, including a lightning outbreak ahead of intensification and a small vortex tilt, and what held it back: dry mid-level air on its east side.

Python · xarray · MetPy · Aircraft & satellite observations

Graduate thesis · Coming soon

Severe Weather and Public Communication in New York City

My graduate thesis project, in collaboration with the National Weather Service New York office (Upton, NY) and New York City Emergency Management (NYCEM), focuses on severe weather in New York City and how it is communicated to the public.
